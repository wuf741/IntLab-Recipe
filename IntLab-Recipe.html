Recipe: How to Get From Parsons UC to the Natural History Museum

Have you ever needed to get from Parsons to the Natural History Museum for a field trip or just for fun? Here's how you do it using the subway system!

Ingredients

You'll need...

  • 1 OMNY Card
  • Phone for a map
  • Yourself
  • Optional: Sketchbook

Instructions

  1. The starting point is in front of the Parsons UC, on 63rd 5th Avenue.
  2. Walk west to the 14 St./8 Ave. Train Station.
  3. Tap your OMNY card.
  4. Take the next uptown C train.
  5. Get off at the 81 St. Museum of Natural History Station.
  6. Walk in the direction of Central Park West until you reach the museum
You've arrived, enjoy your visit!
